Regulatory & Compliance Disclosures

IKAG Capital LLC is a deal origination intermediary and does not provide direct lending services, financial consulting, investment advice, or securities-related services. IKAG Capital LLC is not a registered broker-dealer, investment advisor, or financial institution, and does not engage in activities requiring such registration. All financing facilitated through IKAG Capital LLC is provided by unaffiliated third-party capital partners and is subject to those partners’ credit approval and underwriting requirements. No financing outcome is guaranteed.

IKAG Capital LLC operates in accordance with applicable federal and state regulations. Where FINRA regulations are applicable, IKAG Capital LLC conducts its operations in compliance therewith. IKAG Capital LLC does not solicit or sell securities and does not engage in broker-dealer activities as defined under applicable FINRA and SEC rules.

Performance metrics and network KPIs displayed on this website reflect the aggregate transaction history and capital relationships of IKAG Capital LLC’s advisory partner network. These figures represent collective partner activity and do not reflect IKAG Capital LLC’s independent closed transaction volume.

Nothing on this website constitutes an offer or solicitation to provide financing in any jurisdiction where such solicitation is unlawful.
