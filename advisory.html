Advisory Services

End-to-End
Capital Guidance.

Capital markets advisory goes beyond making introductions. It means understanding the project economics, structuring the optimal capital stack, preparing institutional-quality underwriting, and representing sponsor interests through negotiation.

Capital Stack Advisory

We analyze project economics and capital requirements to recommend the optimal mix of senior debt, mezzanine, preferred equity, and common equity. Balance leverage, dilution, and return targets while advising on recourse trade-offs.

Underwriting & Feasibility

Institutional-grade underwriting and feasibility analysis to validate project assumptions and ensure bankability. Lender-ready models, stress testing for rate and market scenarios, third-party validation.

Deal Packaging

We package and present transactions to our capital partner network with the precision institutional lenders require. Executive summaries, sponsor bios, market studies, rent comps, exit strategy documentation, and coordinated outreach.

Term Sheet Negotiation

From initial term sheets to final loan documents, we represent sponsor interests in structuring and negotiating capital commitments. Term sheet comparison, negotiation of key economic and legal terms, coordination with counsel.

Portfolio & Strategy Advisory

For sponsors managing multiple assets or seeking growth capital, we provide portfolio-level advisory on recapitalization, refinancing, and strategic capital planning. Portfolio valuation, recap timing, long-term capital partner introductions.

The Process

How We Work.

01
Intake

We review the project, sponsor profile, capital needs, and timeline. Every transaction is intake-qualified before it advances into our origination pipeline.

02
Review

Institutional underwriting and feasibility analysis. We stress-test assumptions and validate the deal against the criteria our capital partners apply.

03
Strategy

We design the optimal capital structure and identify the appropriate capital sources. The stack is built to the transaction, not the other way around.

04
Execution

Coordinated lender and equity partner outreach, term sheet negotiation, and due diligence management. We drive the process while sponsors focus on the deal.

05
Closing

Coordination through closing and funding. All outcomes are contingent upon capital partner underwriting and credit approval.

Regulatory & Compliance Disclosures

IKAG Capital LLC is a deal origination intermediary and does not provide direct lending services, financial consulting, investment advice, or securities-related services. IKAG Capital LLC is not a registered broker-dealer, investment advisor, or financial institution, and does not engage in activities requiring such registration. All financing facilitated through IKAG Capital LLC is provided by unaffiliated third-party capital partners and is subject to those partners’ credit approval and underwriting requirements. No financing outcome is guaranteed.

IKAG Capital LLC operates in accordance with applicable federal and state regulations. Where FINRA regulations are applicable, IKAG Capital LLC conducts its operations in compliance therewith. IKAG Capital LLC does not solicit or sell securities and does not engage in broker-dealer activities as defined under applicable FINRA and SEC rules.

Performance metrics and network KPIs displayed on this website reflect the aggregate transaction history and capital relationships of IKAG Capital LLC’s advisory partner network. These figures represent collective partner activity and do not reflect IKAG Capital LLC’s independent closed transaction volume.
